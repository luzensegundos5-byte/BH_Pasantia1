Must-See Historic & Cultural Sites

Explore the heritage of the Americas right outside Billini Hotel.

UNESCO World Heritage

Zona Colonial

Discover historic cobblestone streets, colonial architecture, vibrant squares, museums, and charming outdoor cafes.

Right outside hotel

Catedral Primada de América

The first cathedral built in the Americas, noted for its Gothic-Renaissance style and rich historical artifacts.

3 min walk (300m)

Alcázar de Colón

Former palace of Christopher Columbus’s son Diego, transformed into a museum displaying late medieval and Renaissance artwork.

8 min walk (650m)

Fortaleza Ozama & Parque Colón

The oldest European military fortress in the New World and the lively central gathering plaza.

5 min walk (450m)

Traditional Dominican Dishes

La Bandera Dominicana

The national lunch plate featuring white rice, red stewed beans, seasoned beef or chicken, and fried plantains.

Mangú ("Los Tres Golpes")

Classic breakfast dish of mashed green plantains topped with pickled red onions, fried cheese, salami, and eggs.

Sancocho

A rich, hearty stew cooked with up to seven types of meat, root vegetables, corn, and traditional local spices.

Pollo Guisado

Slow-braised caramelized chicken cooked with bell peppers, herbs, and garlic, bursting with local taste.

Nature & Beaches

Tropical coastlines, limestone caves, and botanical gardens.

Caribbean Beach

Boca Chica & Juan Dolio

Crystal clear shallow waters and white sandy shores located just 30 to 45 minutes drive from the hotel.

35 mins Taxi / Uber
Natural Cave System

Los Tres Ojos National Park

A dramatic 50-foot open-air limestone cave with three crystal freshwater underground lakes.

15 mins drive ~$4 USD entry

National Botanical Garden

One of the largest botanical gardens in the Caribbean, featuring a peaceful Japanese Garden and native orchid house.

Culture & Experiences

Music, art, crafts, and memorable photography spots.

Merengue & Bachata Rhythms

Experience live Dominican music and dancing listed as UNESCO Intangible Cultural Heritage of Humanity.

Larimar & Amber Jewelry

Shop for unique local handcrafted jewelry made from rare turquoise Larimar stone found only in the Dominican Republic.
